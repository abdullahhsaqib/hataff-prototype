OUR PEOPLE / OUR FOUNDATION
ON WATCH SINCE1995

Our people come from the forces. Ex-servicemen and ex-SSG commandos, bringing the discipline of their service to the people who trust us at their gate.

Established on 28 February 1995, Hataff Security Systems protects Pakistan’s businesses, institutions and families. Among the first fifteen security firms granted legal status in Pakistan, our heritage is built on service.

Preventive by design. Ready to act when needed. Quality that is never compromised.
